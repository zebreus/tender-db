**The expected stray rate from dates.**
- In phase A, all **11** after-end mentions (D4U1Y 8, 26A 2, 93C 1) carry the **ended body's own name**. Under ruling
  4 (no date fence) they join P, and the gate admits them. **0 of 11** would mint a stray for a date reason.
- The publishers keep the old name and the old code after the merger: Frimley's name sits on D4U1Y as late as
  2026-10-05.
- Across all classes, exactly **one** after-end mention names the new body: 10Q's "NHS Thames Valley ICB" (06-18).
  Under QU9 the gate refuses it, so it would stand as a stray. 10Q is A3 (U6) in any case.
- RGQ67's site name fails the gate whatever the date (513's "hosted sites stay separate").
- The strays to expect therefore come from **other-body names, not dates.** That is M1's subject. Among the 215
  mentions published since 2026-04-01 the visible cases are:
  - 15N "NHS Bristol, North Somerset and South Gloucestershire ICB" ×2 (a §1.4 rehome candidate);
  - 04Y "NHS Shropshire, Telford and Wrekin ICB" ×1;
  - 11X "Rural Practice Network" ×1.

  **47366322** (D4U1Y, 2026-10-05).
