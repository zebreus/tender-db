# 519 M2: the dated child map (2026-10-12)

Measure-first step M2 of `../unit2-decision.md`. Offline work over ODS ORD records. Nothing under `crates/` was
written: `nhs-ods.tsv` is byte-identical (sha256 `348fbad5…e994`, recorded in `child-map.json`), at HEAD 10ca61a.

## Files

- `fetch_ro98.py`: fetches the ORD record of every RO98 row of `crates/ingest/sdk/nhs-ods.tsv` into `ods-ro98/`
  (wrapped `{code, http, body}` like `../ods/`). 03K is fetched first and never seeded. The other 51 RO98 records in
  `../ods-table-codes/` are seeded from there. The script exits on any status other than 200 or 404, with a 0.5 s
  pause between requests. `--offline` refuses to fetch.
- `ods-ro98/`: 344 records. 293 were fetched today and 51 seeded. All 344 answered **HTTP 200; there were no 404s**.
  Five requests hit a transport reset (curl 35) and succeeded on retry.
- `build_child_map.py`: offline. Reads the RO98 records and the 25 measured RO198 site records
  (`../ods/`, primary role RO198 in `../gbnhs-orgs-classified.json`), and writes `child-map.json`.
- `child-map.json`:
  - `rows`: one per (child, parent, merged Operational interval). Columns are `code, parent, rel, from, to`
    (`to` null means open), plus child status and name, parent role, status, allow flag and name, the Legal
    dates and the relationship status.
  - A child with no qualifying parent gets one row with `rel "-"` and `parent null`.
  - `parents`: children per parent. `no_parent`, `overlaps` and `dropped_relations` hold the report.
  - `holders`: the prod holders per child code (unit 1).

The rule is §2.1/§2.2 of the decision:
- RO98 children keep `Rels.Rel` with `id RE5` whose target's primary role is RO261.
- RO198 sites keep `RE6 → RO197`.
- The dates are Operational. Adjacent intervals with the same parent (end + 1 day = next start) are merged.
