## How they were found

The census lists holder ids only for allowlisted codes. A zero-only tail's holders show up only through `other_codes` on a QHM holder, which gives 3 of the 10. Neither `section_id` nor `raw_identifier` has an index. The other 7 were found in three steps:
1. `name_norm` range seeks on the tails' published names and their spellings (32 prefixes);
2. each candidate's mentions read by `organization_mentions_org`, filtered to `raw_identifier LIKE 'GB-NHS-00%'`;
3. a sibling-notice pass (`tender_versions_notice`, then the `tender_versions` PK), which confirmed no 00P party sat on another holder of the same tenders.

Every census count is accounted for: 00P has 6 holders / 25 parties, 00N 1 / 5, 00L 1 / 1, 00X 2 / 2.
