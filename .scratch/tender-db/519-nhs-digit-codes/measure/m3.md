# 519 M3: purity and flags per child holder (2026-10-12)

This is measure-first step M3 of `../unit2-decision.md`. It counts the holders that E3 and E4 would deny:
- `child-impure`: a mention that does not carry the holder's own triple;
- `child-names-unread`: a capped read;
- `child-carries-ppon`: the holder carries a PPON, as its own identifier or a merged one;
- `child-withheld`: a 452 verdict withholds the holder's triple.

It also checks whether a holder appears in a ledger, a review store or a 469/513 report. The scope is unit 1's 77
child holders (`../gbnhs-orgs-classified.json`, primary role RO98 or RO198): 52 sub-ICB locations and 25 trust sites.
The zero-only clusters (00P, 00N, 00L, 00X) are M5's and are not covered here.

## 1. Raw identifiers on the mentions (`child-impure`)

All 1,880 mentions are FTS, country `GB`, scheme `GB-NHS`. Each one normalises
(`project::normalise_identifier`: ASCII alphanumerics, upper-cased) to the holder's own triple
`(GB, national, GBNHS<C>)`.

| written as | mentions |
|---|---:|
| `GB-NHS-<C>` exactly | 1,783 |
| same triple, other spelling | 97: lower-case tail 96 (`26a` 81, `11x` 4, `36l` 4, `15e` 2, `16c`, `91q`, `92g`, `93c`, `99c` 1 each) and `GB-NHS-16 (c)` 1 |
| country-less | **0** |
| foreign country | **0** |
| another value | **0** |
| no identifier | **0** |

- **Country-less mentions cannot occur at all.** The FTS parser defaults a party's country to GB
  (`crates/ingest/src/fts/parse.rs:216-224`, the default at 220-224). The 513 census agrees: `countryless_parties 0` and
  `non_gb_parties 0` over every GB-NHS coded party.
- **For U4's triple arm.** The lower-case spellings key the same triple, so they bind. 26A writes `26a` on 81 of
  its 115 mentions.

## 2. PPON, own or merged (`child-carries-ppon`)

- **Identifiers.** All 77 carry their own `GBNHS<C>` as their identifier: national, GB, non-provisional.
  `merged_identifiers` is `[]` on all 77.
  - So no holder was the keep of an e2-altid, r2 or r3 merge of an identified loser.
  - No holder has been merged away: all answer 200 under their own id.

  - **06H: 1**. The census does not name the PPON. 469's sample pairs 06H with PNJC6453ZWZN, the PPON of 31535193

  - the other 42 listed three-character codes: 0.
  - Five-character children and sites are not tail-read, so they were not measured this way.
  - Both 06H and 11X are A2 (anchor-held).
- **Bound orgs.** Every listed code has `holders 1`: each code binds exactly one org.

## Caveats

- **Proxies for the ledger.** The ledger itself is not readable. The two proxies are the merged identifiers and
  the org standing under its own id. They cover merges of identified losers. A name-keyed (provisional) loser
  folded into a holder writes no merged identifier. Such a merge would still show up in the mention read
  (section 1), because a loser's mentions repoint to the keep. All 1,880 mentions carry the holder's own triple, so
  none came from an uncoded loser.
- **The `name` readback is truncated** at 5,000 rows, newest first.
