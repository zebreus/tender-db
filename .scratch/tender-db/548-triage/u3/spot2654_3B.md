**20 — 15497200 GCS Achats du Centre (FR buyer, SIRET 13001627200010): N**
- Head: the full form appears in only one place. That is the 2020-07-09 contract notice 320817-2020
  (notice 21134833, content `3B_nc_21134833.xml`), which is version 1 of tender 6834232.
- Two months later the award notice for the same tender (449920-2020, 2020-09-25) prints `GCS achats du centre`.
- The buyer list holds 88 tenders, 2020-09-25 to 2024-08-14. The newest, 760191, prints `GCS Achats du Centre`.
  Spellings: 177 of 178 mentions are GCS-form.
- Verdict: same entity. "GCS" is the abbreviation of "Groupement de coopération sanitaire", so the head
  is the spelled-out form, not a former name. The served form is the body's own spelling in every notice
  since 2020-09. Neither is worse. N (R would be defensible).
