## Side findings that bear on the cause

**Window selection is not on the release `date`.**
- 2,134 monthly members (0.7 %) are dated after their package's month, and none before.
- Example: `000060-2021` sits in 2021-01, tagged `tender`, dated 2021-07-27T18:14:18. Many 2021 members carry 2021-07-27 18:14, which looks like a bulk re-touch.
- So `date` is the latest edit, and the window keys on something earlier. I placed ids in time by the earliest date among each id and all later ids, because a later edit can only push a date forward.

**One id can arrive as two different releases.** 11 ids appear in two monthly packages with different `ocid`s: a `tenderUpdate` on the old procurement and an `award,contract` on the new one, with the same date. Examples are `038018-2025` (in 2025-04 and 2025-07) and `086149-2026` (in 2026-03 and daily 2026-09-10). Inside a single package, `assemble_fts_zip` keeps only the first copy, so the second release would be dropped without trace. That loses releases, not ids.

**The 2025–2026 single ids look different.** The two held ids either side of each are a median of 108 seconds apart (10th percentile 29 s, 90th 390 s). Their neighbours' tags look like the overall mix. They are also lost on page 1 in 2026 (1.0 %), unlike the page-2 losses. They could be ids that were never published, or another paging fault. Each needs checking by id against the API, which I did not do.
